Wushu & Kung Fu

Stances, kicks, jumps, forms and weapons like broadsword, staff, straight sword, fan and 9-section whip chain. Skill, strength and flexibility grow step by step.

Ages 4–7

Little Tigers

A first taste of wushu for young kids: basic moves, balance and listening skills in a fun, positive class.

Ages 7–17

  5. Intermediate IIIBlue-black to red-black

Head coach

Coach Ding Wei

Coach Ding trained on the Sichuan Province Professional Wushu Team from 1986, studying for over ten years under Xiong Chang Gun, “The Monkey King.” He earned a Bachelor of Science in Wushu and Professional Coaching from Chengdu Sports University and competed in three All China Games.

His teams have won in China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Southeast Asia and the US, and he has sent students to the US National Wushu Team many times. He's known for patient, detailed teaching and high expectations for every student, at every age.

  • 2000 China National Champion: straight sword, spear and duilian
  • 2000 Asian Wushu Games Champion
  • 1993, 1997, 2001 All China Games
  • 2005 on Coached students onto the US National Wushu Team
  • 2016 Tiger Claw Monkey King Champion & Chen Tai Chi Champion
  • Credentials 7th Duan, Chinese Martial Arts · USAWKF-certified judge

Our students on the podium

From first-timer trophies to the national team, our athletes compete across the US and around the world.

  • 2025Two athletes selected to the USA National Wushu Team (junior and youth teams).

  • 2025Golden State International Wushu Championships: women's adult all-around grand champion, plus gold in long fist, sword and spear.
